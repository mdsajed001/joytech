## Why

The current "Why Shop With Us?" (Joy Telecom Promise) section takes up too much vertical space on mobile devices. Stacking five large cards with icons, titles, and descriptions creates a wall of content that pushes products down and interrupts the shopping experience without adding sufficient value.

## What Changes

- Redesign the trust points section on mobile to use a dense 2-column grid.
- Hide the descriptions of the trust points on mobile screens, displaying only the icons and titles to save space while retaining the trust signals.
- Maintain the current full 5-column layout with descriptions for desktop and larger screens.
