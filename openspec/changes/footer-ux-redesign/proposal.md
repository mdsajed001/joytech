## Why

The current global footer is overwhelming on mobile devices, stacking multiple sections of links into a very long scroll. Additionally, it lacks crucial social media trust signals and uses overly bright accent colors for section headers, which visually clutters the UI and departs from premium design system standards. 

## What Changes

- Collapse the footer link sections ("Top Categories", "Customer Service", "Policies & Legal") into accordions on mobile to save vertical space.
- Refine typography across the footer: update headings to a neutral `text-white` with `font-semibold` instead of uppercase accent colors, and increase general readability.
- Add social media icons (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp) beneath the contact information to build trust and brand presence.
- Realign payment badges to the right on desktop for a cleaner layout.
